    Maternal equipment and device forecast

    Uses the Facility forecast activity and planning period. These illustrative device categories and capacity assumptions are not a national equipment standard. Validate service packages, peak concurrency and specifications with facility teams before procurement.

    Capacity is events per device per month at full availability, including cleaning and turnaround. Usable capacity accounts for downtime and operating hours. Minimum units reflect simultaneous service readiness; enter locally agreed values, including zero for services not provided. Functional excludes nonfunctional equipment. Due for replacement is a subset of functional units retiring within this planning period. Repairs and confirmed arrivals must be usable before needed.

    Required = max(minimum units, ceiling(monthly eligible events × (1 + peak allowance) ÷ (events per unit per month × usable capacity))). Surviving units = functional − due for replacement + confirmed repairs + confirmed incoming. Procurement gap = max(0, required − surviving units). Nonfunctional units are recorded separately; only confirmed repairs count toward supply. Replacement age and lead time inform manual review and do not automatically create units or orders. Estimate each facility separately before aggregating; pooled country totals conceal local gaps.

    Inventory is blank until entered. Save planning data to retain your work; nothing is sent to a server.
