How the forecast works

Demand

Use a fixed target if you know how many units you need. For a workload forecast, the tool grows the first-year events at the entered rate, applies the eligible share and coverage, then divides by usable capacity or multiplies by units per event for single-use devices. A service-point norm multiplies planned points by units per point.

Supply and replacements

The procurement gap is the required quantity less opening stock, after retirements and confirmed additions, with a floor of zero. Retirements for existing equipment and confirmed additions follow the annual schedule you enter. Projected reusable purchases retire after the entered useful life. Single-use devices are deducted when used. A missing or inconsistent opening balance makes the procurement gap unknown for that year and later years.

Costs

Cost estimates use the same entered landed unit cost in every year. Costed subtotals omit purchases without a unit cost or valid currency. Record the data source, readiness constraints and validation owner for each item.
